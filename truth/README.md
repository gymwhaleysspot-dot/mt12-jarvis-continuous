# Truth Authority

Truth is the protected evidence layer for Jarvis, Nickelle, and Michael.

Rules:
- Owner measurements and owner photographs are highest authority.
- Research evidence must retain source, confidence, timestamp, and contradictions.
- Generated renders and model predictions never overwrite owner truth directly.
- Jarvis may validate and reconcile Truth; Nickelle may read it and propose additions; Michael consumes it.
- Any contradiction is preserved until explicitly resolved by stronger evidence.
